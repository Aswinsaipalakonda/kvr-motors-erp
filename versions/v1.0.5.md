## Core Modules & Shared Capabilities Across All Roles

Every role (**Owner**, **Branch Supervisor**, **Sales Executive**, **Tele Caller**) is provisioned with five standard base modules across web and mobile apps:

1. **Dashboard**: Role-tailored operational metrics, daily targets, and action shortcuts.
2. **Stock Inventory View**: Real-time listing of **Available Vehicles** and **Stock-Out / Sold Vehicles** within the user's assigned branch.
3. **Attendance Module**: Live hardware GPS & selfie clock-in / clock-out, shift tracking, and monthly logs.
4. **Profile Module**: User identity, role details, branch assignment, and password management.
5. **Invoice Download Engine**: Search and PDF invoice download access for all closed sales within their branch scope.

## End-to-End Workflow Architecture

### 1. Master Vehicle & Battery Mapping (Owner Only)
- **Centralized Master Management**: Owner creates Vehicle Models and Battery Models in the system master catalog and maps compatible batteries directly to vehicle models.
- **Automated Branch Distribution**: Mapped vehicle configurations automatically display in respective branches based on physical stock allocations without requiring branch staff to manage battery logistics separately.

### 2. Enquiry Creation with Vehicle Interest (Tele Caller)
- Tele Callers record customer enquiries with name, phone number, interest status (`INTERESTED`, `UNINTERESTED`), and notes.
- **Stock-Aware Vehicle Selection**: Tele Caller selects the customer's interested vehicle model from live branch inventory.
- **Lead Archival**: Uninterested or dropped enquiries are assigned a mandatory `LOST` classification reason (e.g., *Price High*, *Competitor Choice*, *Finance Rejected*) and archived.

### 3. Booking & Stock Reservation (`HOLD` Trigger)
- Sales Executive converts active Enquiry into a **Booking**.
- **Stock Hold Constraint**: Stock numbers update and vehicle unit status transitions to **`HOLD`** **ONLY WHEN** an advance payment or full payment transaction is recorded.
- Without payment confirmation, the vehicle unit remains in `AVAILABLE` stock.

### 4. Sale Submission & Customer Mobile Lookup (Sales Executive)
- **Mobile Number Lookup**: Sales Executive enters the customer's 10-digit mobile number in the Sale module. The system auto-populates all enquiry, booking, customer identity, and payment breakdown details.
- **Sale Submission**: Sales Executive verifies final order parameters and submits the sale. The transaction status changes to **`PENDING_VERIFICATION`**.

### 5. Payment Verification & Sale Closure (Supervisor / Owner)
- **Closure Authorization Gate**: Only Supervisors or Owners can review pending sale requests.
- **Payment Audit**: Supervisor/Owner verifies advance and final payment receipts (Cash, Bank Transfer, UPI, Finance Disbursal).
- **Sale Closure**: Upon approval, status changes to **`CLOSED`**, and vehicle VIN and battery serial numbers transition to **`SOLD`**.

### 6. Universal Invoice Download Access
- Upon sale closure, an official PDF sales invoice is locked and stored.
- **All Roles** (Owner, Supervisor, Sales Exec, Tele Caller) can search, view, and download the PDF invoice directly for customer delivery.

## Mobile App API Specification & Data Model Contracts

### 1. Common Modules Endpoint Specifications
- `GET /api/v1/auth/profile/`: Fetches user profile, role, branch assignment, and permission scopes.
- `GET /api/v1/attendance/status/`: Fetches today's attendance status and clock-in logs.
- `POST /api/v1/attendance/clock-in/`: Submits selfie image + GPS coordinates (`latitude`, `longitude`).
- `GET /api/v1/inventory/branch-stock/`: Returns branch vehicle stock split into `available_units` and `stock_out_units`.

### 2. Enquiry & Lead Management Endpoints
- `GET /api/v1/crm/enquiries/`: List branch enquiries with status filters (`NEW`, `IN_PROGRESS`, `CONVERTED`, `LOST`).
- `POST /api/v1/crm/enquiries/`: Create new enquiry including `customer_name`, `phone`, `interested_vehicle_id`, `source`.
- `POST /api/v1/crm/enquiries/{id}/mark-lost/`: Transition enquiry to lost with compulsory `lost_reason` text.

### 3. Booking & Advance Payment Endpoints
- `POST /api/v1/crm/bookings/convert/`: Converts `enquiry_id` to booking.
- `POST /api/v1/crm/bookings/{id}/record-payment/`: Records advance payment, updates stock unit status to **`HOLD`**, and logs payment method details.

### 4. Mobile Search & Sale Handover Endpoints
- `GET /api/v1/crm/sales/lookup/?mobile={phone_number}`: Fast indexed search returning customer identity, active booking, held vehicle VIN, and payment summary.
- `POST /api/v1/crm/sales/submit/`: Sales Executive submits completed order parameters (Sets status to `PENDING_VERIFICATION`).
- `POST /api/v1/crm/sales/{id}/verify-and-close/`: Supervisor/Owner endpoint to approve payment and mark sale as `CLOSED` (Sets vehicle status to `SOLD`).
- `GET /api/v1/crm/sales/{id}/invoice/pdf/`: Generates / downloads binary PDF invoice (Accessible by all branch roles).
